Valerie Mirto has provided transformational coaching for women in business since 2000. With a focus on authenticity and Success Through Self-care™, she combines high-impact change strategies with mindfulness tools to help women create the lives they want to be living.

After more than two decades, Valerie remains passionate about the power of coaching to change lives. She designs her programs and services to champion alignment, expanded thinking, and the incalculable value of treating yourself beautifully.

She loves working with women who want to stear off the burnout path and build lives and businesses they love. Clients say they appreciate her warm, holistic approach, paired with an emphasis on doable approaches. 

When she's not coaching or writing, she is likely in her art studio or traveling the world with her family.
